Bridging data
and decision-making.

I'm a Data Analytics student at Miami University with a deep focus on sport analytics. I'm driven by a simple idea: better data leads to better decisions, whether that's a coach setting a lineup, an executive evaluating a trade, or a manufacturer optimizing a workflow.

As a Performance Analytics Manager for Miami Redhawks Softball, I work directly with coaches using tools like Trackman, Synergy, and AWRE to shape game strategy through opponent scouting and lineup optimization. Analytics isn't just academic to me — it's applied daily in a real competitive environment.

On the industry side, I'm currently a Data Analyst Intern at Dynamic Metal Services, where I convert raw lab outputs into clean, decision-ready datasets and automate data workflows using the Dropbox API and Power Query.

I'm a National Merit Commended Student and proud member of the Honors College. Outside the classroom, I run events for the Miami Sport Analytics Club and serve as Treasurer of the Golf Club.
